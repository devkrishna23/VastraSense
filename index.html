Clothes labelling

VastraSense · your 300 real phone photos of clothes · these labels teach the app's model
  1. Type: choose the category, then the type of the main garment in the photo.
  2. Colour: the colour that covers the largest area of the garment, as it would look in daylight. Add a second colour only if it covers a big part. Use also possible when you are torn between two colours, and the rainbow tile for multicoloured prints.
  3. Use Swatches or the Wheel; on the wheel, Pick from photo samples the garment's real colour.
  4. If you cannot tell what the photo shows, press Ambiguous photo and save.
  5. Your answers are saved on this device as you go. When you stop, press Send my labels and send the file to Krishna.
Answer what you see; there are no hints on purpose. About 10 seconds a photo.
Continuing on another device? Pick your name, then use Load in the top bar with the file you sent earlier.
